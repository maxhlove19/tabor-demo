Privacy

The short version

Everything you do in this app stays on your phone. We do not have an account system, we do not collect your name or email, and we do not sell or share anything, because what you do in the app never leaves your phone.

What is stored on your device

Your lesson progress, quiz scores, notes, highlights, saved verses, prayer entries, walking history and settings. Deleting the app deletes it from your phone. You can also erase it any time in Settings.

Prayers and notes

Your prayer journal and Bible notes are never uploaded. Nobody but you can read them.

Steps and motion

When step counting arrives, the app will read your step count only to move you along a walk. It is used on your device only and is not stored on any server.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your card details. We receive only an anonymous confirmation that a subscription is active.

Ads and tracking

There are no ads and no advertising trackers in this app.

Children

The app is written for teens and adults. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Questions

Contact details will be listed on the App Store page for this app.
